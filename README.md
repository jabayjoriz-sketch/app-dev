# My Favorite Movie
## Movie Name: **The Conjuring**

**Genre:** Horror, Supernatural, Thriller  
**Rating:** 10/10

### Why I Like It
- The storyline is very interesting and suspenseful  
- The characters are well-developed and engaging  
- Keeps you on the edge of your seat  
- Great mix of horror and mystery
  
### Main Characters
1. **Ed Warren** – Paranormal investigator  
2. **Lorraine Warren** – Psychic and investigator  
3. **The Perron Family** – The family experiencing supernatural events
